# Persona Extraction and Category-Conditioned Generation

We constructed the active persona resource by normalizing 31,733 source profiles (31,577 Cactus records and 156 CBT-DP reference records) while retaining their source narratives, communication features, provenance, and licensing metadata, and then assigning each profile exactly one of six crisis categories with the revision-pinned Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct classifier using semantic fit and harm direction rather than lexical overlap. Because the source pool contained fewer than 100 sufficiently diverse profiles for self-harm, suicidal ideation, and violent thoughts, we used `gpt-4o-mini-2024-07-18` to generate non-graphic category-conditioned adaptations from distinct base-persona families; each adaptation preserved stable identity attributes, was explicitly marked as constructed rather than source-observed, and was required to specify a coherent current, enacted, or historical harm direction. We conservatively restored source-only adjacent reassignments to their original anxiety category and selected replacements only from a larger candidate pool that passed an item-level independent audit of category coherence, harm-direction coherence, identity continuity, provenance accuracy, and non-graphicness. The resulting sidecar contained 31,046 anxiety-crisis, 102 risk-taking, 101 self-harm, 216 substance-related, 168 suicidal-ideation, and 100 violent-thought profiles; all 268 constructed rows passed all five audit dimensions, and the three augmented categories each contained 100 unique base families. Finally, we sorted records by `persona_id` within category and exported six non-overlapping JSONL partitions whose union exactly reproduced the canonical 31,733-row sidecar, with per-partition counts and SHA-256 checksums recorded in `data/personas/by_category/index.json`.
